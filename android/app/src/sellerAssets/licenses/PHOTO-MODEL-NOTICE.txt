ManchApp seller photo editor

Image processing library: OpenCV Android 4.12.0, distributed from the official Maven artifact org.opencv:opencv.
Source and license: https://github.com/opencv/opencv/tree/4.12.0

Segmentation architecture and pretrained source: U2NetP / U^2-Net by Xuebin Qin and contributors.
Original project: https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
Original license: Apache License 2.0 (included as U2Net-APACHE-2.0.txt).

ONNX conversion distribution: rembg by Daniel Gatis and contributors.
Source: https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/blob/main/rembg/sessions/u2netp.py
Model URL: https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/releases/download/v0.0.0/u2netp.onnx
Converter license: MIT (included as rembg-MIT.txt).
Verified MD5 from distributor: 8e83ca70e441ab06c318d82300c84806
Packaged model SHA-256: 309c8469258dda742793dce0ebea8e6dd393174f89934733ecc8b14c76f4ddd8

The model is bundled only in the seller application and runs locally on CPU.
No learned food colour LUT is included. Natural colour correction uses conventional OpenCV processing.
